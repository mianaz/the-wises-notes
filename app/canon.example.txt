贤者笔记 canon file: template
================================
The easy way: run ./scripts/fetch-canon.py, which builds app/canon.txt
for you, then ./scripts/deploy.sh --canon to copy it to the tablet.

By hand: copy this file to canon.txt in the app folder on the tablet
(/home/root/xovi/exthome/appload/sage-notes/canon.txt), then delete
this header and fill it with the exact lines you want Hroptatyr to be
able to quote word for word. When you ask for exact words
(「原话是什么」, "what were your exact words"), the notebook quotes from
here verbatim. Anything not in this file, he paraphrases and never invents.

PERSONAL USE ONLY. These lines are HoYoverse's copyrighted script text.
Keep canon.txt for your own notebook; never share, commit or redistribute it.

Where the lines come from:
  中文: https://wiki.biligame.com/ys/被镌刻的时光   (section 任务剧情)
  EN:   https://genshin-impact.fandom.com/wiki/Etched_in_Time  (Dialogue)

Tips
- Keep only the lines you care about: his own dialogue, the lines said to
  him, and the notebook entries.
- By default (SAGE_CANON_MODE=lookup) the file never goes to the model:
  only the scene headings do, and the notebook finds the line itself. With
  SAGE_CANON_MODE=inline the whole file rides along with every page, which
  is slower and dearer (the full quest is roughly 10k tokens).
- Label each scene so he knows when and to whom a line was said.
- Keep both languages if you like; he picks the one you wrote in.
